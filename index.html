About me

In 2024 I was invited to join TikTok’s Trust and Safety Advisory Council for sub-Saharan Africa. This engagement became an eye-opener to the scale at which violent extremists and other criminal actors exploit social media platforms for communication, propaganda, radicalisation and recruitment of predominantly young people. My past research already informed me of these dynamics but now I have a deeper insight regarding the multiplier effect of these AI-powered platforms.

My professional journey in this field makes me aware that while violent extremism is an “old enemy”, it can no longer be fought with only boots on the ground. This adversary now has digital footprints reflected in algorithms and trends. Addressing this adaptive challenge requires professionals who can combine their expertise of a longstanding subject matter with the contemporary application of AI governance. This is where I bring value.

By bridging geopolitical risk analysis with digital and algorithmic accountability, I empower organisations to anticipate emerging risks and implement robust compliance strategies.
