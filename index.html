Executive Profile

Peace, Security & AI Governance Specialist

Dr. Akinola Olojo is an internationally recognized Peace, Conflict Management, and AI Governance Specialist with over 16 years of strategic experience bridging high-level security policy, counter-terrorism research, and modern technological risk architecture.

In his current advisory capacity, Dr. Olojo serves as a member of TikTok’s Safety Advisory Council for Sub-Saharan Africa, providing expert risk assessment frameworks to counter malicious online actors, threat models, and algorithmic dissemination of extremist content. He regularly conducts AI regulatory mapping, NIST AI Risk Management evaluations, and threat mitigation models for global law firms, engineering entities, and diplomatic bodies worldwide.
